# FinSight AI — Project Overview

## 1. Project Name

FinSight AI

## 2. Project Type

Intelligent FinTech Operations & Risk Intelligence Platform

## 3. Overview

FinSight AI is an AI-powered platform designed to assist fintech
operations, risk, support, and finance teams in analyzing
transaction data, detecting anomalies, investigating payment issues,
understanding enterprise documents, and generating insights.

The platform combines traditional Machine Learning, Large Language
Models, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Document Intelligence,
and AI Agents.

## 4. Problem Statement

FinTech organizations generate large amounts of transactional,
customer, merchant, payment, refund, chargeback, and operational data.

Important information is also distributed across policies,
technical documentation, operational procedures, and other business
documents.

Teams often need to manually investigate this information across
different systems.

FinSight AI aims to provide a unified platform where structured
fintech data and unstructured business knowledge can be analyzed
using AI.

## 5. Objectives

The main objectives are:

1. Predict transaction risk using Machine Learning.
2. Detect unusual transaction and merchant behavior.
3. Process and understand enterprise documents.
4. Build a RAG-based knowledge assistant.
5. Develop an AI investigation agent.
6. Enable natural-language analytics over fintech data.
7. Assist with chargeback investigations.
8. Generate AI-assisted investigation reports.
9. Provide secure role-based access.
10. Evaluate and monitor AI system performance.
