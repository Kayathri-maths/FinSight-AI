# FinSight AI

## Intelligent FinTech Operations & Risk Intelligence Platform

FinSight AI is an AI-powered fintech operations platform that combines:

- Machine Learning
- Anomaly Detection
- Document Intelligence
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- Large Language Models (LLMs)
- AI Agents
- Natural Language Analytics

The platform is designed to help fintech operations and risk teams
analyze transactions, detect anomalies, investigate issues,
understand company documents, and generate AI-assisted insights.
